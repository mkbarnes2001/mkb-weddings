Latest gate: **v1.10.15a production deployment PASS, 6 September 2026**. Schema 54 is live; booking setup and Calendar are released with public booking closed until provider acceptance. ## Agreed scope

Both instant confirmation and approval requests are selectable per service. Individual team calendars are included from the first version. Clients can pay a deposit or the full amount during booking.

- WedCRM Settings → Online booking has Sessions, Add-ons, Availability, Client info and Payment steps. Sessions include images, descriptions, prices, durations, preparation/cleanup buffers, staff, optional extras and Job type. Order and availability are editable. Image upload reuses the existing workspace-scoped catalogue image service.
- A saved, enabled booking page exposes `/book/{business-slug}` with copy/preview controls. It runs outside professional authentication. Clients choose the session, extras, staff/time, enter their details and consent, then review the total and payment due. No client identity is falsely marked verified.
- WedCRM Calendar has month, week and agenda views, staff filtering, time off, approval, decline, cancellation and rescheduling. Existing dated Jobs appear as all-day entries and block matching staff; unassigned dated Jobs block the business. Online appointments create existing CRM Leads, contacts, Jobs and (when priced) invoices, without inventing a Wedding record for non-wedding services.
- Operational Admin actions use the existing 32 × 32 square controls and accessible hover/focus labels. Step navigation, record selection, calendar entries and public booking controls retain readable labels.

## Booking and payment integrity

Reservations and their Lead, Job, contact links, invoice, schedule and activity are one database batch. Database overlap and settings-revision checks prevent concurrent callers from creating conflicting or orphaned records. Staff-linked resources are unique per workspace member, and inactive memberships cannot receive new appointments. Existing Job editors cannot independently move or delete an appointment's scheduling history. Manual all-day Job creation/update also respects reserved calendar time.

The displayed settings revision must still match when a client reserves. A price or deposit change requires a fresh review. Amounts, duration, service, add-ons, staff, consent and terms are snapshotted server-side; browser-submitted prices are never used. Prices are final customer totals; this version does not add automatic tax calculation. Supported booking currencies are GBP, EUR, USD, CAD, AUD and NZD, using two decimal minor units.

Paid reservations hold the slot for 40 minutes. Checkout expires after 35 minutes and is created promptly after reservation. A deterministic payment attempt/idempotency key prevents repeated checkout clicks from creating duplicate obligations. The same immutable amount, currency, connected account and invoice schedule are used by the existing signed Stripe settlement processor. The return URL cannot settle money or confirm a booking. Stripe documents a minimum Checkout expiry of 30 minutes; the shorter initial creation window accommodates that constraint. [Checkout expiry](https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create)

Deposits are a percentage of the session plus chosen extras, rounded up to the nearest minor unit. The invoice retains a separate remaining-balance schedule due on the appointment date. Very small payments are rejected before reservation; Stripe's settlement-currency rules remain authoritative. [Minimum amounts](https://docs.stripe.com/currencies#minimum-and-maximum-charge-amounts)

An unpaid expired hold cancels its provisional Job and voids the unpaid invoice. A verified late payment can reopen only the void invoice identified by the trusted saved online-booking attempt; account, amount, currency and ownership validation still run first. Money is recorded in the original ledger and the appointment enters **Payment needs review**, rather than taking a potentially reassigned slot. Partial payments after expiry also require review. Approval requests remain provisional after payment until a professional approves them.

Cancelling or declining preserves financial history and does **not** automatically refund a card payment. The Admin and public views explain that payments/refunds need business review. Existing invoice payment receipts remain behind the verified-payment workflow. Booking confirmations are now a separate, configurable message with the same guarded delivery/outbox infrastructure; they require explicit deployment activation. Calendar sync sends no invitations. No real email was sent during either local gate.

## Google Calendar

Each booking resource can connect a Google primary calendar through a separate server-side OAuth flow. State is single-use, expires after ten minutes, and is bound to the professional user, membership and workspace. PKCE is used. Refresh credentials are encrypted with AES-GCM and bound to the workspace/resource; no credential is returned to the browser. The flow requests `calendar.events` for event reads and writes, plus `calendar.calendarlist.readonly` for choosing conflict calendars. Existing connections need fresh consent to load that selector. [OAuth web-server flow](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/web-server), [Calendar authorization](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/auth)

External busy intervals are checked live when selecting/reserving a slot and when confirming/rescheduling. If a connected calendar cannot be checked, availability fails closed. External busy time can be shown in the CRM calendar without exposing event descriptions. Confirmed/requested online bookings and blocked time are written to Google without attendees or invitation emails. Changes belong in WedCRM; edits to Google events are not imported as CRM booking changes. Existing legacy all-day Jobs are displayed/checked locally but are not exported as new Google events in this version.

Stable Google event IDs, retained target links, a workspace sync lease and version-checked completion support retries and remove an old staff calendar entry on reassignment. Errors remain visible for retry from Calendar; Calendar loading and successful booking mutations also request a sync. A disconnected Google calendar may retain its previous entries. [Calendar event insertion](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/events/insert)

## Apple iCloud extension

**Local extension gate PASS — 5 September 2026. Real iCloud account acceptance remains pending.**

Calendar → Calendar connections now groups Google Calendar and Apple iCloud. Each active team member can discover and select one editable iCloud calendar, reconnect with a replacement app-specific password, or disconnect. A calendar cannot be assigned to two staff members in the same workspace. Only the selected calendar supplies busy times; this does not aggregate every calendar in an Apple account.

The connection uses Apple Account email and an app-specific password, which requires two-factor authentication. The normal Apple password is not accepted by the connection form. Apple also offers account authorization for supported third-party apps, but this implementation does not claim enrollment in that program. Password-based CalDAV is the implemented path. [Apple app-specific passwords](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102654), [Apple third-party authorization](https://support.apple.com/en-us/121539)

Credentials are encrypted with the existing deployment credential key, with separate iCloud/workspace/staff authenticated encryption context. Google encryption compatibility is preserved. Passwords are transient in the form, cleared on completion/cancel/unmount, and never returned by the Admin API, put in URLs, or written to browser storage. Authentication and CRM management permission are required; same-origin mutations and HTTPS protect the connection endpoints. Discovery and redirects are restricted to known Apple CalDAV hosts before credentials are sent. Responses are size/time bounded and XML namespace-aware; external entities and malformed XML are rejected.

Busy checks join Google and iCloud for slot selection, reservation, payment reconciliation, confirmation and rescheduling. CalDAV reports request server-expanded recurring events, with date-only/floating events interpreted in the selected calendar's timezone (business timezone when the calendar supplies none). Recurrence that was not expanded, unsupported timezone data, ambiguous wall times and provider failures stop availability from being advertised. Event titles and personal details are not exposed by the external-busy response. [CalDAV recurrence expansion, RFC 4791 §9.6.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4791.html#section-9.6.5)

Confirmed/requested online bookings and time off export to iCloud. Stable event identifiers and conditional writes support retry; only our own event UID can be overwritten or removed. Entries with externally added attendees/organizers are left for review. There are no attendee invitations, organizer fields or mail sends. Staff reassignment removes the previous destination before settling sync status; version checks keep concurrent edits pending. An iCloud sync lease also guards connection replacement/disconnection. Existing all-day Jobs are still checked locally and not exported. Changes to CRM appointments must be made in WedCRM; this does not import iCloud edits as CRM appointment changes. Sync runs on the same booking mutations, Calendar loads and manual retry as Google, with bounded batches; background push sync remains outside this candidate.

Disconnect deletes the locally saved credential and stops checking that calendar. Existing exported entries remain in iCloud; the inline disconnect control states this. Choosing a different destination requires disconnect first. A professional can separately revoke the app-specific password in their Apple Account.

The candidate storage adds iCloud connection/link records and sync status inside the **same unnumbered proposal**, only applied to isolated SQLite databases. No schema metadata or numbered migration changed. `CRM_CALENDAR_CREDENTIAL_KEY` and the existing online-booking feature flag are required; no Google OAuth client is needed for iCloud. Pinned dependencies `saxes` 6.0.0 and `ical.js` 2.2.1 provide XML/iCalendar parsing and serialization.
